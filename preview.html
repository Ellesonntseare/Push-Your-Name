PUSH YOUR NAME

Development preview for the TikTok effect. Push each letter onto a matching outline. You can push, but cannot pull.

Use arrow keys to try the puzzle. Camera is off.

Head controls: face the camera, press Centre head, then turn left/right or look up/down. Return to centre between moves.

Recording saves a silent WebM of the game and camera image. Camera frames are processed in your browser. Head tracking needs an internet connection to download its model.
